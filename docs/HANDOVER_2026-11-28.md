## What Phase 8 contains (uncommitted)

1. **Live session limits.** Without these, live video likely drops after about 2 minutes and any connection after about 10.
   - `src/utils/liveSession.ts` (+ 44 tests) and `netlify/functions/_shared/live-session-config.ts`: context-window compression (trigger 50,000 tokens, target 25,000), `sessionResumption: {}`, and a reconnect controller.
   - Reconnect: on `goAway` or an unexpected close, resume with the saved handle. It tries the same token first, then a fresh token (one more quota unit). At most 3 consecutive attempts, with the budget reset after 30 s of stable connection.
   - `LiveVideoCopilot.tsx` and `LiveExpertCall.tsx` are wired to it. Old-socket callbacks are ignored through `connGenRef` plus `sessionIdRef`.
   - `_shared/live-token.ts` locks compression into the ephemeral token. If Google rejects that field mask with a 400, it retries once without the lock. `sessionResumption` is NOT locked, so the client can pass a handle.
   - Unverified: real Live behaviour, including whether Google accepts the compression field mask and whether a resume works on the same token.
2. **Farm profile on the server.**
   - `src/services/farmProfile.ts` (+ tests), `src/hooks/useFarmProfileSync.ts` and `src/components/FarmProfileSync.tsx` (mounted at the top of `Layout.tsx`'s JSX).
   - Mirrors lang, mainCrop, place, location (3 decimals) and cropPlan to `users/{uid}/farm_profile/main` with a full `setDoc`.
   - Restores on a new device only when the device has no answers. It never writes before a successful read. The device wins on conflict, and writes are debounced by 2 s.
3. **Farmer context and memory** (not yet used by any endpoint; Phase 9 will use them).
   - `_shared/farmer-context.ts` (+ 50 tests):
     - `FarmerFacts` and `formatFarmerContext(facts, lang)`, capped at 6,000 characters. It is honest about unknowns and quotes farmer text so it cannot inject instructions.
     - `loadFarmerFacts(uid, {adminFetch, provided})` reads the farm profile, dossier, seasons and money, plots, the last 3 diagnoses, the last recovery note, open tasks, memory and area summaries.
     - Weather, rules and price are passed in by the caller.
   - `_shared/assistant-memory-model.ts` (+ tests): limits and validators.
   - `src/services/assistantMemory.ts` (+ tests): client functions to list, add, update, deactivate and delete facts.
4. **`firestore.rules`.** One new block (about 160 lines, near the end) for `users/{uid}/farm_profile/main`, `assistant_memory`, `assistant_threads` and `messages`. All are owner-only with strict shapes; the owner may also delete threads and messages. **It has not been tested in an emulator.**
5. **`firestore.indexes.json`.** New index on `diagnoses` (`userId` ASC, `createdAt` DESC). The loader falls back without it.
6. **Clean-ups.**
   - `src/nav/tabs.ts` `partitionTabsByMode` plus `Layout.tsx`: in Simple mode the drawer groups the extra tools under "আরও সেবা".
   - Deleted the dead `generateWeatherAdvisory`, `findGovernmentSchemes` and `getMarketInsightsByLocation` from `src/services/ai.ts`.
7. **Admin AI model check.**
   - `POST /api/ai-model-check`: `netlify/functions/ai-model-check.ts`, `_shared/model-check.ts` (+ 19 tests), a `server.ts` route, and an `AiModelCheckCard` on the AdminDashboard "command" tab.
   - Admin is the verified admin email, the same rule as `firestore.rules`.
   - It runs 6 tiny probes:
     - text on 3.5-flash-lite, 3.6-flash, 3.8-flash and 2.5-flash
     - Google Search on 3.6-flash
     - TTS on 3.8-flash-lite-tts
   - Purpose: confirm whether 3.8 Flash and Search grounding really fail on the real key, as old code comments claim.
   - `server.ts`'s `callFetchHandler` now forwards the `Authorization` header.
